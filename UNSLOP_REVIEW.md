# Patient interface polish

Direction (existing brand retained; user requested direct optimization):
- Product: Thyroid postoperative patients follow previously approved rehabilitation guidance.
- Character: Calm, familiar, legible.
- World: A hospital nursing instruction sheet used on a patient's phone.
- Moments: The supplied hospital emblem and the currently selected three-step movement.
- Signature: Five short safety questions, one at a time; the emergency help stays reachable.

Audit: the previous UI exposed five safety groups, technical AI explanations, repeated limitations, long navigation labels and several concurrent tasks. The new draft also had inconsistent 10/14/18px gaps and over-bold branding.

Polish mode keeps the selected A palette, hospital emblem and four destinations. The patient's short-reading requirement authorizes the progressive disclosure and question flow; no new clinical capability or clinical claim was added.

Passes: spacing follows a 4px rhythm; one calm palette and one action accent; no decorative icons or emoji; everyday short copy; 17px patient body copy and restrained weights; one question and main action at a time; technical detail and references remain accessible; the real hospital emblem is preserved; motion is limited to control feedback and respects reduced motion.

No image generation or story video was needed. Browser verification uses the available CUA browser rather than a second browser automation stack.
